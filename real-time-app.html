A real-time application built in Unreal Engine that simulates a complete residential environment — not a single render, but a living space you can explore from any angle, at any season and time of day.

  1. Drone view ↔ ground view with a seamless transition
  2. MetaHuman walkthrough at 1:1 scale, interiors included
  3. Surroundings & amenities system with live distances
  4. Ultra Dynamic Sky — 4 seasons, weather and time of day
  5. Teleport, cinematic camera and screenshot tools
  6. Animated traffic, construction zone, EV charging, playgrounds
