Swiss Archviz

3D Visualization Lachin Guluyev
Year 2026
Software 3ds Max, Corona Renderer, Photoshop, ComfyUI

Modern Residential Visualization | Exterior Concept in Switzerland

This project reflects a balance between modern architecture and a calm, livable environment. Clean geometry, soft tones, and natural surroundings were combined to create a space that feels both elegant and comfortable.

My role in this project focused on bringing the design to life through visual storytelling:
– 3D Visualization & Rendering
– Lighting Setup & Atmosphere
– Material Creation & Detailing
– Corona Renderer Workflow
– ComfyUI-assisted visual enhancement
– Strong focus on realism and composition

The goal was not just to show a building, but to create a feeling — a place where people can imagine their daily life.
